# Scripture research, track 5c results: collapsed manuscript trees (2026-10-20)

Track 5c, part 1, of the class A programme, run against the pre-registration
in [scripture-research-class-a.md](scripture-research-class-a.md) (track 5c,
part 1), which was committed before this run. It re-scores
[track 5](scripture-research-results-stemma-2026-10-20.md)'s builders after
contracting every branch the data does not support, then re-reads the same
pinned NTVMR transcriptions (CC BY 4.0, Institut für Neutestamentliche
Textforschung; `data/scripture/research/ntvmr.tsv`, no new download). The
generated report is reproduced in full below the reading. Reproduce it (about
10 seconds, after track 5's first fetch) with:

```bash
cargo run --release -p flock-api-server --bin flock-scripture-import -- research stemma-collapsed
```

## Reading

* **The builders are now validated.**
  * Collapsed neighbour joining and collapsed parsimony both score a mean
    normalized RF of 0.004 at kappa = 0, against the registered bar of 0.25.
    Binary, the same trees scored 0.272.
  * They keep every true split (recall 1.000) and almost nothing else
    (precision 0.99). Track 5's diagnosis was right: its whole error was the
    splits a binary tree must invent.
  * The diagnostic at the application's own witness counts agrees: 0.006 at
    21 observed witnesses and at 8.
* **Contamination is the limit, and the real tradition is contaminated.**
  * Collapsed RF rises to 0.15-0.17 at kappa = 0.05, about 0.2 at 0.1 and
    0.30-0.32 at 0.2. Recall stays high and precision falls to about 0.56 at
    0.2: under mixture, the builders invent supported but false groups.
  * The bar was registered at kappa = 0, and the builders pass it there.
    How contaminated the early Greek witnesses are is not measured here.
    Read the application with that in mind.
* **The real trees lose nothing to the collapse.** Every internal branch of
  both builders' trees is supported, for both passages: 18 of 18 for Mark and
  5 of 5 for John. So the collapsed trees are track 5's trees, and every
  root tally below is track 5's figure. What has changed is their status:
  **the application is no longer descriptive-only** for either builder.
  * A check that was not registered (it is not in the report): the weakest
    kept branch carries 2 changes in Mark and 13 in John. That is the
    length increase from contracting that branch alone, on either builder's
    tree.
* **Mark 16:9-20: presence at the root is the more parsimonious history, on
  validated trees.**
  * 01 and 03 lack it, and 19 placed witnesses have it.
  * Under equal costs, 36 of the 39 root positions favour presence, and so
    does the midpoint root (1 change against 3). When loss is cheaper than
    gain, all 39 do.
  * On both trees, 01 and 03 form a group of their own, so one loss explains
    both.
  * This counts events on a tree of 21 witnesses. It gives no weight to how
    old 01 and 03 are. Part 2 of this track registers a model that does.
* **John 7:53-8:11: still the cost switch decides, now on validated trees.**
  * Six of the eight placed witnesses lack it: P66, P75, 01, 03, 038 and 33.
  * Under equal costs, every root position that is not a tie favours
    absence, and so does the midpoint root (2 changes against 4).
  * When loss costs a third of gain, every position that is not a tie
    favours presence, and so does the midpoint root (5 against 6), by one
    unit of cost.
  * The two witnesses that have it do not form a group of their own on
    either tree.
  * With eight witnesses the method still cannot separate the hypotheses.
    The validation shows the trees are right. It cannot supply the missing
    witnesses.

## Deviations and post-run changes

None. The rule, bar, diagnostics and application ran exactly as registered.
The only code change outside track 5c is a refactor: the passage summary and
root table were moved into shared helpers. Track 5's report
(`research stemma`) was regenerated after it and is byte-identical to the one
in its results doc.

## What would change the answer

* **A measured contamination level.** If the early Greek tradition sits
  near kappa = 0.2, the builders are back above the bar. Estimating kappa
  from the data, for example from the share of units that conflict with the
  tree, would be a new pre-registration.
* **More witnesses**, as in track 5, especially for John.
* **The witnesses' dates**, which part 2 of this track registers.
