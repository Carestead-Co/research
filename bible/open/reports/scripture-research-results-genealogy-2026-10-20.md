## Reading

* **Validated.** Aligning Matthew's kings (Matt 1:7-11) with the kings of
  Judah in 1-2 Kings finds exactly Ahaziah, Joash, Amaziah and Jehoiakim
  omitted, with no false positive. 1 Chr 3:10-16 agrees.
* **Every hypothesis is consistent.** Each covers every father conflict the
  data contains:
  * Joseph's father;
  * Zerubbabel's father;
  * Luke 3:33's Admin and Arni against Ram;
  * under "same men", Shealtiel's father.
  * Two kinds of conflict are explained by omitted generations instead:
    Matthew's omissions, and Genesis 11's lack of Luke's Cainan.

  So consistency does not decide between them, which is the expected answer.
* **Assumption count.** H4, a royal-legal line in Matthew and a natural line
  in Luke with two different Shealtiels, needs the fewest: 10. H1 and H2 need
  11 under "different men". H3, which merges the Shealtiels, needs 14, and
  H1 and H2 need 15 under "same men". Merging the Shealtiels and Zerubbabels
  always costs four assumptions: two identities, a second father for
  Shealtiel, and Rhesa as an unlisted son. These are counts, not
  probabilities. The model claims nothing about which story is historically
  true.
* **Generation spans: the pre-registered prior is miscalibrated.**
  * Normal(30, 7) puts the attested royal line, 18 generations from David to
    Jeconiah in 1 Chr 3 over the same 403 years, at p = 2e-7. Real
    generations in this line averaged 22.4 years.
  * So the prior's "implausible" verdicts on Luke do not stand. Luke's 42
    generations from David to Jesus average 23.7 years, close to the
    attested royal line.
  * Matthew's third section is implausible on any reasonable prior: 13
    generations over about 592 years, 45.5 years each. It is the one finding
    the calibration does not undercut: Matthew's third fourteen omits
    generations, as his second demonstrably does.

## What would change the answer (a new pre-registration, not a retune)

* **A generation prior calibrated on attested lines** before it scores any
  hypothesis, such as the Judahite royal line dated by track 3a or other
  Near Eastern dynasties.
* **Anchor uncertainty as a distribution, not a point,** since David's dates
  and Jesus's birth carry decades of uncertainty.
