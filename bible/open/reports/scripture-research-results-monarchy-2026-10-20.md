## Reading

* **The solver is not validated, so no date below is a finding.** It fails
  the bar at every pre-registered cost setting.
  * **c_emend = 1** comes close. It recovers 100% of chronologies with one
    corruption and 91% with two, against a bar of 95%, and its flags are 97%
    precise.
  * **c_emend = 3 and 10** fail for one structural reason. A corrupted
    synchronism costs c_emend to emend. Moving that king's start instead
    costs a co-regency on one side and an interregnum on the other, 1 + 1 = 2.
    So the solver invents a co-regency pair rather than emend, recovers 78% of
    single corruptions, and flags the wrong items a third of the time.
  * The pre-registered ratio, co-regencies cheap against emendations, is
    exactly the one that hides a textual error behind invented overlaps.
* **Descriptively, the counting switch decides feasibility before the costs do.**
  * Zedekiah's accession in 597 BC and his 11th year in 587 BC fit only
    non-accession counting in Judah.
  * 586 BC fits only accession counting.
  * So half the profile and anchor pairs are infeasible outright.
* **Descriptively, the feasible least-cost solutions put Rehoboam's accession
  between 930 and 938 BC.**
  * Thiele's profile and the Israel non-accession / Judah accession profile
    give 931 BC, Thiele's own date. The literal 1 Kgs 6:1 Exodus then falls
    in 1446 BC.
  * Every one of these optima is tied with 20 to 64,416 others of the same
    cost, so a single date is not determined even within the model.
* **Both unsteered statements are unmet in every solution.**
  * 2 Kgs 15:30 dates Hoshea by Jotham's 20th year. Jotham reigned 16.
  * 2 Chr 16:1 has Baasha alive in Asa's 36th year. By Kings, Baasha died
    before Asa's 26th.
  * The data notes record both tensions.

## What would change the answer (a new pre-registration, not a retune)

* **A cost ratio that does not reward inventing overlaps,** for example
  c_coregency = c_gap = 2 with c_emend = 3. Then shifting a start costs more
  than emending one statement. It needs its own synthetic bar, registered
  before it runs.
* **Tie reporting by range.** Report the earliest and latest Rehoboam date
  over all tied optima, not one representative. That would show whether the
  ties move the anchor date at all.
* **Half-year calendars** (Nisan and Tishri years, as Thiele used). The
  integer-year model cannot express them, and they are the usual explanation
  of off-by-one synchronisms.
